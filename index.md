<p>I am a second-year PhD student in the <strong>Department of Computational and Data Sciences</strong> at the <strong>Indian Institute of Science (IISc) Bangalore</strong>, working at the intersection of mathematics, computation, and quantum information theory under the guidance of <strong>Dr. Ratikanta Behera</strong>. My research focuses on <strong>tensor decompositions</strong>, <strong>machine learning</strong>, and their application to solving high-dimensional PDEs and quantum computation problems. I hold a <strong>Junior Research Fellowship (JRF)</strong> supporting my doctoral work. Outside of research, I enjoy baking, drawing, and reading manga.</p>
